# Yeongsu-Harry-Lee

Yeongsu (Harry) Lee is a Global MBA candidate at the Shidler College of
Business, University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa, with a Bachelor of Laws from Keio
University. He brings more than five years of experience at Google and P&G,
specializing in consumer insights, strategic planning, marketing, sales, and
cross-functional leadership across Japan and South Korea. This repository
captures his professional background, strategic analysis, and capability-
building engagements.
