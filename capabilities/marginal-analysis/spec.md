# Marginal analysis specification

## Purpose

Estimate the incremental benefit and incremental cost of a decision relative
to a clearly stated baseline.

## Required inputs

- Baseline volume, revenue, and cost
- Proposed volume, revenue, and cost
- One-time investment and recurring costs
- Decision horizon and currency
- Explicit assumptions for uncertain inputs

## Outputs

- Incremental revenue
- Incremental cost
- Incremental contribution
- Contribution margin change
- Break-even volume or price
- Sensitivity to the highest-impact assumptions

## Guardrails

- Keep baseline and proposed cases comparable.
- Separate one-time from recurring effects.
- Show assumptions rather than hiding them in formulas.
- Label estimates and sourced values distinctly.
- Do not treat the model as a forecast without validating the assumptions.
