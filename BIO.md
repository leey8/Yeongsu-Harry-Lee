# Professional Biography: Yeongsu (Harry) Lee

## Executive Summary

**Yeongsu (Harry) Lee** is a Global MBA candidate at the Shidler College of Business, University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa. He holds a Bachelor of Laws degree from Keio University in Japan and brings a rich multicultural background spanning Japan and South Korea. Harry has over five years of proven experience at leading global corporations, including P&G and Google. Specialized in consumer insights, strategic planning, marketing, and sales, he possesses strong expertise in leading cross-functional and multicultural teams. He is passionate about driving organizational growth, fostering cross-border collaboration, and developing innovative business strategies in global markets.
